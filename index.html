Help & Usage Guide

Short guide to the tools available in MC CUTEX.

1. Basic app usage

Choose Steel Cutting, Steel Weight, Price Estimate or Saved Calculations from Home. Enter dimensions, units and quantity, then use the page’s calculation controls.

2. Weight and cutting methods

Weight Calculator estimates theoretical steel weight using steel density of 7,850 kg/m³ and the selected profile’s dimensions. Cutting Calculator uses the entered stock weight per foot, cut lengths, quantities, scrap rate and entered costs. Results are estimates; compare commercial weight with supplier or scale data.

3. Price and scrap estimate

The cutting estimate uses the app’s current calculation result. In Price Estimate, estimated total payment is calculated from entered/calculated weight × customer rate, plus additional charges. Cutting results also show leftover scrap and its value when those inputs apply.

4. Share a customer quotation

Open Price Estimate, enter party, product, quantity, weight, rate and any additional charges. Review the quotation preview, then use the image download/share controls or WhatsApp message option available on that page.

5. FAQ

Are calculated weights exact commercial weights?

No. Weight Calculator provides theoretical estimates. Actual weight may vary by product tolerance and supplier practice.

Where are saved estimates stored?

Saved estimates use this browser’s local storage on this device. Clearing browser data may remove them.

Can the app work offline?

The app shell may open offline after it has been cached. Features depending on browser sharing or uncached assets may still require browser support.

About MC CUTEX

MC CUTEX is a Steel Business Management Software developed by M Corporation to simplify everyday steel business operations through practical digital tools.

Designed with a focus on steel cutting, MC CUTEX brings essential tools for cutting calculations, weight estimation, price estimation, and customer quotation into one convenient platform.

Our goal is to make steel-related calculations and estimation workflows simpler, faster, and more convenient for steel traders and businesses.
